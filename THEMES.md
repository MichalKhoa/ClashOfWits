# ⚔️ Clash of Wits - Battle Themes

This document lists all the battle themes currently available in the **Clash of Wits** Discord bot. When a duel or Battle Royale is initiated with themes enabled, one of these themes is randomly selected. The AI uses the environment and its rules to shape the battle narrative.

| ID | Theme Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cyberpunk Neon Grid** | A rain-slicked, neon-lit alleyway in a futuristic megacity. Tech-glitches, cybernetics, and hacking are highly effective. |
| 2 | **Medieval Dragon's Lair** | Inside a volcanic cavern filled with piles of gold and a sleeping dragon. High stakes, magic, and chivalry rule the day. |
| 3 | **Deep Ocean Trench** | Under crushing water pressure surrounded by bioluminescent creatures. Movement is extremely slow, and water-based logic applies. |
| 4 | **Kitchen Counter Brawl** | Shrunk down to 1 inch tall on a busy kitchen counter. Utensils, ingredients, boiling pots, and toaster heat are deadly hazards. |
| 5 | **Low Gravity Moon** | On the dusty lunar surface. Jumps fly hundreds of feet high, impact is slow, and there is no air to carry sound. |
| 6 | **High Noon Saloon** | A dusty wild west town at exactly 12:00 PM. High tension, dramatic pauses, tumbleweeds, and quick-draw speed are key. |
| 7 | **Glitchy Retro Game** | An 8-bit platformer filled with lag, pixelation, random power-up blocks, floating platforms, and lava pits. |
| 8 | **Disco Inferno** | On a glowing dance floor under a giant disco ball. Combatants must dance to fight, and style points are factored into power. |
| 9 | **Microscopic Petri Dish** | Battles occur at the cellular level. Giant amoebas, bacteria, and white blood cells roam the arena like wild beasts. |
| 10 | **Mad Max Wasteland** | A scorched desert with scrap-metal cars, rusted spikes, roaring engines, and sandstorms. |
| 11 | **Silent Film Era** | The battle is black-and-white, slapstick-heavy, accompanied by a fast-paced piano track. Speeches are displayed on title cards. |
| 12 | **Unstable Dimension** | Every few seconds, the laws of physics and gravity invert. What was once up is now down, and objects randomly change weight. |
| 13 | **Giant Toy Box** | In a messy kid's playroom. Cardboard castles, building blocks, rubber ducks, and action figures serve as weapons or terrain. |
| 14 | **Candyland Sugar Rush** | A land made entirely of sweets. Sticky marshmallow bogs, sharp candy canes, soda geysers, and extreme sugar rushes. |
| 15 | **Ancient Roman Colosseum** | Before thousands of cheering spectating citizens. Gladiatorial weapons, lion cages, and satisfying the crowd are essential. |
| 16 | **Haunted Victorian Mansion** | Creaking floorboards, floating candelabras, and shifting paintings. Ghostly encounters, possessions, and spooky illusions are highly effective. |
| 17 | **Prehistoric Dinosaur Jungle** | A humid, ancient jungle dominated by towering ferns and active volcanoes. Beware of roaming T-Rexes, falling meteors, and primitive weapons. |
| 18 | **Corporate Boardroom** | A sterile, high-stakes meeting room of a mega-corporation. Synergies, leverage, buzzwords, PowerPoint slides, and coffee spills are lethal weapons. |
| 19 | **Space Opera Fleet Battle** | Fighting in the vacuum of space between two colossal capital ships. Blaster fire, shield configurations, gravity wells, and dramatic monologues are key. |
| 20 | **Magical Girl Anime Stadium** | A sparkling, colorful arena filled with rainbows and glitter. Attacks must be shouted in long, overly-dramatic magical phrases with transformation sequences. |
| 21 | **Pirate Galleon in a Maelstrom** | On the deck of a wooden galleon caught in a massive swirling whirlpool. Cannons, swinging ropes, sea shanties, and slippery decks dictate the battle. |
| 22 | **Cyber-Garden Greenhouse** | A giant glass dome filled with genetically engineered hyper-intelligent plants. Carnivorous vines, glowing pollen, and photosynthesis boosts are in play. |
| 23 | **Viking Fjord Snowstorm** | A freezing, blizzard-swept fjord. Frozen weapons, frostbite hazards, loud war cries, and warm mead power-ups are common. |
| 24 | **Antique Library of Secrets** | A labyrinth of towering bookshelves with magical books. Shushing librarians, paper cuts, flying spellbooks, and literal word-play are dominant. |
| 25 | **Retro-Futuristic Steampunk Airship** | A massive, brass-and-wood airship soaring through the clouds. Steam-powered gadgets, clockwork gears, soot clouds, and structural damage hazards abound. |
| 26 | **Inside a Living Motherboard** | Combatants are digitized and fight on a green motherboard circuit. Antivirus bots, logic gates, firewalls, and data packets can be weaponized. |
| 27 | **Dream Realm of Whimsy** | A bizarre dreamscape where thoughts manifest instantly. Logic is optional, colors change based on mood, and pillow fights can be devastating. |
| 28 | **Haunted Amusement Park** | A decaying theme park at midnight. Creepy clown animatronics, runaway roller coasters, hall of mirrors reflections, and cotton candy traps. |
| 29 | **Comic Book Action Bubble** | Every physical impact spawns giant, stylized sound effects ('POW!', 'BAM!', 'ZOOM!'). The hero or villain with the most dramatic backstory gets a stat boost. |
| 30 | **Cooking Show Kitchen** | A high-pressure television cooking set. Combatants must prepare a culinary masterpiece while fighting, using spices, frying pans, and chef critiques as weapons. |
